MVP MultiPlan

Designing planning logic flexible enough to encode each customer's priorities — shape, clarity, weight, and inclusion strategy — into one configurable system.

StatusIn progress
MethodCustomer research-led
My roleInitiative lead

Context

MultiPlan plans several polished stones out of a single rough rather than one. But different manufacturing customers optimize for fundamentally different things — one prioritizes weight retention, another chases specific shapes, a third protects against particular inclusions or clarity outcomes.

The problem

A single fixed planning logic can't serve customers with conflicting priorities. The product has to be configurable — but configurability easily becomes complexity, and planners won't adopt a tool that asks them to manage dozens of settings to get a sensible result.

My role & approach

I'm leading the initiative end to end:

  • Running customer research across major manufacturing clients to map how each one actually makes planning decisions.
  • Designing configurable logic for shape combinations, clarity priorities, weight optimization, inclusion strategies, and customer-specific workflows.
  • Defining the right configuration primitives — the smallest set of controls that flex to each customer without overwhelming the planner.
